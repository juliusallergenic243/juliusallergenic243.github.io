⚡ Get Started with bruv

🔍 How It Works — In Plain Language

bruv is a type of program called a Command-Line Interface (CLI). You interact with it by typing text and pressing Enter. Here's the simple process:

  1. You provide data — This could be a sentence, a list, numbers, or a simple question about something.
  2. bruv picks a model — By default, it uses Jev or Simple Jev, which are free and run locally. If you want more power, you can add your OpenRouter key to use models like Pi or others.
  3. You get a verdict — bruv analyzes your input and outputs exactly one of three things: yes/no, a choice (e.g., "Option A"), or a score (e.g., "7 out of 10").

Example: You type: "Compare these two job offers. Offer A: high salary, long commute. Offer B: lower salary, remote work."
bruv responds: "Choice: Offer B — better work-life balance outweighs salary difference."

🌐 Unlock More Models with OpenRouter

While bruv works perfectly out of the box using Jev and Simple Jev, you can connect it to OpenRouter — a service that gives you access to hundreds of AI models from one simple API. This means:

  • Use specialized models for better accuracy on specific types of data.
  • Access the latest cutting-edge open models as soon as they're released.
  • Compare results from different models to get a more confident answer.

To enable this, you'll need a free OpenRouter account and an API key. Once you have it, simply type /config in bruv and paste your key. That's it!

🖥️ Why Does bruv Use a Command Window?

Don't be scared of the black window! Using a command-line interface (CLI) has major benefits:

  • Speed: It uses less memory and starts up faster than any graphical app.
  • Transparency: You see exactly what's happening — no hidden processes in the background.
  • Scriptability: Advanced users can create batch files to automate their workflow.
  • Lightweight: Perfect for older PCs or machines with limited resources.
